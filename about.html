★ about this site
_□×

debbyOS is a personal corner of the internet built with plain html, css and javascript. no frameworks, no build step, no tracking scripts. just windows, pastel colors and a few games.

the retro desktop look is a love letter to early-2000s personal sites and my endless need to organize things into little windows.

everything here is hand-made and constantly under construction. if something looks broken, it probably is. sorry!
